JDL-STAP Faculty Demo — Zybo Z7-10 + PmodCLS
The demo synthesizes one target at a time across five antenna channels and
eight pulses. A two-beam spatial processor is followed by an 8-point radix-2
Doppler FFT and a peak detector. The PmodCLS shows status, angle, Doppler bin,
and a scaled complex FFT coefficient.
The scenarios cycle through DETECTED, NOT DETECTED, FAR and CLOSER. FAR and
CLOSER are demonstration labels, not radar range measurements. A 60-degree
synthetic target is deliberately outside the two supported steering directions.
The system does not include a physical RF/ADC input.
